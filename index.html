折叠逃生 · 1.0.0 · 生效日期 2026-10-21

玩法与设备

横屏游玩。支持铰链输入的折叠设备可正常半合后展开起跳；其他设备可选择“触控模式”，按住蓄力、松手起跳。开合能力取决于设备与系统。使用自然、轻柔的开合动作，不需要用力挤压或快速反复开合。

收集的金币仅用于本局每 5 枚自动补充 1 格护盾，不可购买、转让或兑换现金。护盾无法抵挡岩浆；小、中、大落石分别消耗 1、2、3 格。

本地数据与隐私

游戏无需账号、无需联网；不包含广告、分析、跟踪 SDK 或应用内购买。层数、金币纪录、当前进度和声音偏好保存在设备本地。支持的铰链状态、角度及计时信息仅用于本地游戏交互，不发送给开发者或第三方服务器。不访问相机、麦克风、通讯录或定位。

删除应用或在系统设置中清除应用数据可删除本地存档；备份副本由操作系统及你的备份设置管理。开发者没有服务器端游戏存档，也无法远程读取或恢复你的纪录。

Privacy and support

Fold Escape is an offline game with no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking SDKs or in-app purchases. Game progress, records and sound preferences stay on your device. Supported hinge input and timing information are used locally and are not transmitted to the developer. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Delete the app or clear its data to remove local saves; manage backup copies through your OS. We hold no server-side gameplay saves. If you email support, we use your address, message and optional attachments only to respond and investigate. You may request deletion by emailing yuansnail@icloud.com. Email providers and the public website host process connection data under their own policies. No advertising or analytics tools are added to the support website.

Play in landscape. Use gentle fold/unfold gestures on supported foldable devices, or hold and release the on-screen button in touch mode. Collected coins automatically replenish shields within the current run and have no cash value. Store purchases and refunds are handled by the relevant platform. Developer: XIAOJUAN YUAN.
